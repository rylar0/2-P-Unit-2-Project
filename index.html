The Owl House Glyphs

The Owl House is a TV show explores an interesting magic system in which elements can be channeled through paper drawings called glyphs.

The main character Luz, on her pursuit to becoming a witch, discovers these glyphs that help her use magical powers despite not being born with them.

The four most important glyphs are directly linked to the Titan.

Light Glyph

The light glyph is the first glyph introduced into the series. It creates a ball of light which helps lessen effects of other spells.

Ice Glyph

The second glyph discovered, it creates a block of ice.

Plant Glyph

Grows a plant.

Fire Glyph

Generates fire.
