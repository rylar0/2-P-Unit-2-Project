The Owl House Glyphs

The Owl House is a TV show explores an interesting magic system in which elements can be channeled through paper drawings called glyphs.

The main character Luz, on her pursuit to becoming a witch, discovers these glyphs that help her use magical powers despite not being born with them.

The four most important glyphs are directly linked to the Titan.

Light Glyph

The light glyph is the first glyph introduced into the series in episode 4. It creates a ball of light which helps lessen effects of other spells. Luz struggles facing an enemy, she recalls her training to help this spell come to light.

Ice Glyph

The second glyph discovered, it creates a block of ice. When Luz was tasked with discovering another spell, she struggled and got her friends in danger, once she finally realized how to help is when the spell revealed itself.

Plant Glyph

Found in the bud of a flower, this glyph grows a plant.

Fire Glyph

This glyph generates a fire spell.
